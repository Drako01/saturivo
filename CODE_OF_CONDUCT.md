# Saturivo Code of Conduct / Código de conducta

## Our commitment / Nuestro compromiso

Saturivo welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Participation in issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions and official project channels must be respectful, constructive and based on technical merit.

Saturivo recibe contribuciones de personas con distintas trayectorias y niveles de experiencia. Esperamos un trato respetuoso, profesional y colaborativo en issues, PRs, revisiones y demás espacios oficiales.

## Expected behavior / Conductas esperadas

- Discuss code, evidence and ideas rather than attacking individuals.
- Offer reproducible examples and actionable, respectful feedback.
- Help newer contributors and avoid exclusionary or dismissive behavior.
- Respect privacy, attribution, intellectual property, licenses and security boundaries.
- Disagree constructively, acknowledge errors and keep discussions relevant.

Se espera dar comentarios fundamentados, colaborar con quienes recién comienzan, respetar la privacidad y argumentar desacuerdos sin ataques personales.

## Unacceptable behavior / Conductas no aceptadas

Harassment, discriminatory comments, threats, personal attacks, intimidation, sexualized remarks, doxxing, publishing private code or credentials, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

No se toleran el acoso, la discriminación, las amenazas, la intimidación, los ataques personales, la exposición no consentida de datos privados ni la publicación de credenciales o código propietario.

## Scope and enforcement / Alcance y aplicación

This policy applies wherever someone participates in or represents the Saturivo project. Maintainers may ask for changes, moderate content, close disruptive threads or restrict participation when appropriate, with proportionate action and fair consideration of context.

Rige para los espacios oficiales del proyecto y quienes lo representen. Los mantenedores pueden moderar, solicitar correcciones o restringir participaciones según la gravedad de la situación.

## Private reporting / Reportes privados

Report conduct concerns through [ArmoTuSitio support](https://armotusitio.com.ar/soporte/) with subject **Saturivo — Code of Conduct**. Provide relevant evidence and links, but do not include unnecessary personal details. Reports will be reviewed discreetly; absolute anonymity cannot be guaranteed.

Para reportar incidentes, usá el [soporte de ArmoTuSitio](https://armotusitio.com.ar/soporte/) indicando **Saturivo — Código de conducta**. No publiques el incidente en un issue si contiene datos privados.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
